Gnome Rogue (Assassination), step by step from level 1 to 60

01
1. Class, spec and race
Stealth past unwanted mobs straight to quest objectives and lock down targets with stuns and Combo Point finishers.
Gnome racials
• Escape Artist: Removes all movement impairments and grants immunity to them for 3 sec. Cooldown: 120 sec.
• Eureka!: Non-periodic damage/healing abilities: Energy, Mana or Rage cost −10%; damage/healing +10%. Also benefits Power Word: Shield; no charges. Duration: 8 sec. Cooldown: 120 sec.
• Engineering Specialization: Engineering devices fail or backfire 20% less often.
• Expansive Mind: Maximum Mana +5%.

05
5. Which skills to use, in order
Opening
1. Stealth + Cheap Shot / Garrote
Open from Stealth with 4s stun and 2 Combo Points
One target
1. Sinister Strike / Backstab
Primary Combo Point builder
2. Slice and Dice -> Eviscerate
Keep Slice and Dice active, then 5-point Eviscerate
Several targets
1. Blade Flurry + Adrenaline Rush
Cleave two targets simultaneously with double Energy regen
When things go wrong
1. Vanish / Evasion / Blind
Drop combat immediately with Vanish or dodge with Evasion
06
6. Levelling zones and dungeons
Lv 1–12
Alliance: Elwynn Forest / Dun Morogh / Teldrassil · Horde: Durotar / Mulgore / Tirisfal Glades
Lv 10: First Talent point unlocks + Complete your Class Quest (Pet / Voidwalker / Stance / Totem) and train First Aid + 2 Primary Professions.
Lv 12–22
Alliance: Westfall / Loch Modan / Darkshore / Redridge · Horde: The Barrens / Silverpine Forest / Stonetalon
Dungeons: Ragefire Chasm (13–18) · Deadmines (17–24) · Wailing Caverns (17–24)
Lv 18–20: Collect your first blue dungeon weapons (Deadmines / WC) and unlock Lv 20 mobility & AoE abilities.
Lv 22–32
Alliance: Duskwood / Wetlands / Ashenvale / Hillsbrad · Horde: Hillsbrad Foothills / Thousand Needles / Ashenvale
Dungeons: Shadowfang Keep (22–30) · Blackfathom Deeps (24–32) · Stockade (24–32)
Lv 30: Unlock mid-tree key talents; start saving 90–100 Gold via gathering professions for your Lv 40 Mount.
Lv 32–42
Alliance: Stranglethorn Vale / Desolace / Arathi Highlands · Horde: Stranglethorn Vale / Desolace / Badlands
Dungeons: Scarlet Monastery: GY, Lib, Arm, Cath (28–42) · Razorfen Downs (37–46)
Lv 40 POWER SPIKE: Unlock your 60% Mount + 31-Point Capstone Talent (Ice Barrier / Mortal Strike / Shadowform / Bloodthirst)!
Lv 42–52
Alliance: Tanaris / Feralas / The Hinterlands / Searing Gorge · Horde: Tanaris / Feralas / The Hinterlands / Searing Gorge
Dungeons: Zul’Farrak (42–50) · Maraudon (46–54) · Sunken Temple (50–56)
Complete Zul’Farrak (Carrot on a Stick mount speed trinket) and Maraudon Princess quest rewards.
Lv 52–60
Alliance: Un’Goro Crater / Western & Eastern Plaguelands / Winterspring · Horde: Un’Goro Crater / Western & Eastern Plaguelands / Winterspring
Dungeons: Blackrock Depths (52–60) · LBRS/UBRS · Scholomance · Stratholme · Dire Maul
Lv 55–60: Complete Onyxia & Molten Core Attunements and gather your dungeon gear for raids.
