Battle Shout (+AP) & Sunder Armor (-2,250 Armor)
Main tank for bosses: Shield Slam, Shield Wall, Last Stand and strong single-target threat.

Blessing of Sanctuary, Kings & Salvation (-30% Threat)
Holds groups of enemies with Consecration and Holy Shield, and brings Blessing of Sanctuary.

Feral Druid (Bear form)

Porta all’incursione
Leader of the Pack (+3% Melee/Ranged Crit) & Faerie Fire
Large health and armor pool, Maul and Swipe for threat, plus Innervate and Rebirth.

Holy and Discipline Priest

Porta all’incursione
Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit & Power Infusion
Flexible healing: Flash Heal, Greater Heal, Prayer of Healing, and magic and disease dispels.

Guaritore

Holy Paladin

Porta all’incursione
Greater Blessings (Kings / Might / Wisdom / Light)
Strong single-target healing; Illumination returns mana on critical heals, and Cleanse removes magic, poison and disease.

Guaritore

Restoration Shaman

Porta all’incursione
Windfury Totem, Mana Tide Totem & Strength of Earth
Chain Heal keeps groups alive; Windfury and Mana Tide Totems help the party.

Restoration Druid

Porta all’incursione
Gift of the Wild (+Armor/Stats/Resists) & Innervate
Rejuvenation, Regrowth and Swiftmend keep tanks topped up; Innervate and Rebirth help the whole raid.

Grows strongest with buffs and raid weapons: Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Heroic Strike and Execute.

Combat and Assassination Rogue

Steady single-target damage with Slice and Dice, Blade Flurry for two targets, Vanish to drop threat.

Retribution Paladin and Enhancement Shaman

Hybrid melee damage that also brings strong buffs: auras for the Paladin, totems for the Shaman.

Frost and Fire Mage

Arcane Brilliance (+Intellect), Conjured Water & Decurse
Strong ranged damage on one or many targets with Frostbolt, Fireball and Ignite; brings Arcane Brilliance and Remove Lesser Curse.

Destruction and Affliction Warlock

Marksmanship Hunter

Lista per la sera dell’incursione

Professioni e accampamento
CAMP
Camp ready
A cook builds the campfire; bring what your professions need to place their camp objects.
WORLD BUFFS
World buffs
Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer, Spirit of Zandalar, Songflower Serenade and the Dire Maul buffs, if your raid uses them.
FLASKS & FOOD
Flasks, elixirs, weapon oils and protection potions
Greater Fire, Shadow or Nature Protection Potions, Mongoose or Greater Arcane Elixir, Mageblood, Nightfin Soup or Blessed Sunfruit, and Dark Runes.
ATTUNEMENTS
Molten Core, Onyxia’s Lair and Blackwing Lair attunements
“Attunement to the Core”, the Drakefire Amulet for Onyxia, and “Blackhand’s Command” for Blackwing Lair.
ADDONS
Boss timers and threat meter up to date
Check that your boss timer, threat meter and heal prediction addons work in the current game version.
GEAR
Repaired gear and enchants
Repair to full, check your enchants, and bring Aqual Quintessence to douse the Molten Core runes.
