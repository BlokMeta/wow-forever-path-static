Cosa è cambiato in Forever

Shaman Class Deep Dive & Nov 10 Class Tuning

6 nov 2026
Per Blizzard's November 10, 2026 class tuning, Enhancement Shaman is tuned so Strength and Attack Power are always its strongest primary stats, while Intellect and Spell Power serve as secondary hybrid stats via Mental Dexterity and Mental Quickness (collect Strength/AP gear rather than Mage/Warlock caster gear). Totem global cooldown is 1 sec; lasting buff totems last 5 min. Weapon imbues last 60 min; Windfury and Flametongue Totem are party buffs; Fire Nova is now a spell.
