Racials

Blood Fury

Changed from Classic
Attack Power and Spell Power +10%. Duration: 15 sec. Cooldown: 120 sec.
Active · Instant · 2 min cooldown
Changed in Blizzard's notes of 26 Oct 2026. Orc Blood Fury's cooldown is reduced to 2 minutes (was 3 minutes).
In Classic: Increases base melee attack power by 25% for 15 sec and reduces healing effects on you by 50% for 25 sec.

Shatter Curse

New in Forever
Dispels Curses and Banes; magic damage taken −5%. Does not grant immunity. Duration: 8 sec. Cooldown: 180 sec.
Active · Instant · 3 min cooldown
Changed in Blizzard's notes of 15 Oct 2026. Orc Shatter Curse now dispels Curses and Banes (it granted immunity), and magic damage taken is reduced by 5% (was 15%).

Command

Passive
Same as Classic
Hunter and Warlock pet damage +5%.
Changed in Blizzard's notes of 26 Oct 2026. Orc Savage (1% increased critical strike chance) is removed; Command is re-added, increasing Warlock and Hunter pet damage by 5%.

Hardiness

Passive
Changed from Classic
Stun duration on you −20%.
In Classic: Chance to resist Stun effects increased by an additional 25%.
