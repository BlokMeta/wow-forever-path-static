World PvP Hotspots & Forever 1.60.1 Ruleset Notes

Cross-Ruleset Battleground Queues & Hardcore Restriction
In World of Warcraft: Forever, Normal (PvE) and PvP ruleset players share unified Battleground queues for fast pop times. Hardcore characters cannot queue for Battlegrounds unless they permanently convert to a Normal ruleset character.
◆
Blackrock Mountain (BRM): Level 55–60 chokepoint leading to Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, BRD, and UBRS — Mind Control lava throws and raid-vs-raid clashes.
◆
Gurubashi Arena (Stranglethorn Vale): Free-for-all chest spawns every 3 hours (00:00, 03:00, 06:00, 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00, 21:00 server time) toward Arena Grand Master trinket.
◆
Tarren Mill vs. Southshore (Hillsbrad Foothills): Classic tug-of-war skirmishes between levels 25 and 60.
◆
Eastern Plaguelands & Silithus: High-level Rich Thorium, Black Lotus, and world objective control battles.
